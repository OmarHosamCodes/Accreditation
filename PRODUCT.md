# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Auditors use the browser extension while reviewing live Instagram and Facebook brand pages. They need to inspect page content, attach evidence to visible page elements, score 16 accreditation metrics, and publish audit results without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Accreditation is a live brand-audit tool. The extension lets auditors evaluate evidence where it appears, link observations to rubric metrics, and publish results into the Accreditation leaderboard.

## Brand Personality

Precise, critical, efficient. The interface should feel like a serious audit instrument, not a marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Do not make the extension feel like a landing page, decorative overlay, or chat widget. Avoid oversized panels that block the social page, hover-only controls, tiny tap targets, and modal-heavy flows.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the audited page visible.
2. Put the next audit action within one tap.
3. Make evidence capture work without mouse assumptions.
4. Use restrained, high-contrast product UI.
5. Preserve desktop behavior while adapting mobile structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use practical WCAG AA standards: readable contrast, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets, 16px form text on mobile, reduced-motion support, keyboard operability where the browser exposes it, and clear status/error text.
